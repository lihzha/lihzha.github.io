We introduce EmbodiedSWE, a framework for using coding agents to solve long-horizon, dexterous robotics tasks and turn verified solutions into scalable training data. EmbodiedSWE-Bench evaluates agents across 28 tasks and 17 robot embodiments, while EmbodiedSWE-Gen diversifies their solutions into demonstrations that improve robot policy learning and generalization.
